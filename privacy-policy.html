Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Andrea Accident ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through andreaaccident.com (the "Site"), how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the Site, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Who We Are

Andrea Accident is a case review and attorney-matching service. We are not a law firm, and we do not provide legal representation or legal advice. We help connect people who have been injured with independent, licensed personal injury attorneys.

2. Information We Collect

When you submit a case review request or contact us through the Site, we may collect:

  • Your full name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Case type and details about what happened, as you choose to share them
  • Your preferred method of contact

We also automatically collect limited technical information when you browse the Site, such as your approximate location (derived from IP address), device and browser type, and pages viewed, through standard web analytics and advertising tools described below.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your case review request
  • Connect you with an independent personal injury attorney who may be able to assist you
  • Contact you by phone, text message, or email regarding your inquiry
  • Improve the Site and our services
  • Comply with legal obligations

By submitting your phone number, you consent to receive calls and text messages from Andrea Accident and/or attorneys we connect you with, regarding your case review request, which may be sent using an automatic telephone dialing system. Consent is not a condition of any service. Message and data rates may apply. You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP.

4. How We Share Your Information

We share your information with:

  • Independent attorneys and law firms in our referral network, so they can evaluate and potentially assist with your case
  • Service providers who help us operate the Site and process submissions, such as our form-processing provider (Formspree) and website hosting provider (Vercel)
  • Advertising platforms, such as Meta (Facebook/Instagram), when you interact with our ads, to help us measure ad performance and reach people likely to need our services

We do not sell your personal information to unrelated third parties for their own marketing purposes.

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The Site may use cookies and similar tracking technologies, including the Meta Pixel, to understand how visitors use the Site and to measure the effectiveness of our advertising. These tools may collect information about your device and browsing behavior. You can control cookies through your browser settings, though disabling them may affect how the Site functions.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, including maintaining records of case referrals, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

8. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information. To make a request, contact us using the information below. We will respond in accordance with applicable law.

9. Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed to individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

11. Contact Us
